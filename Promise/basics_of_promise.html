A Promise represent a way of handaling asynchronus operation is More Organizing Way. It serves the same purpose as a callbacks. It serves the same purpose as callback but offers many additional capabillities and more readable Syntax.

A Promise in JavaScript is a placeFolder for a future value or a action. By Creating a Promise, you are essebtially telling the JavaScript Engine to "promise" to Perform a Specific action and notify you once it is Completed or fails.

The Constructor takes a Single argument: a function called executor.

Promise.then() takes two arguments, a callback for success and another for failure.
